From Mac

Omarchy is aimed at you. DHH left macOS, 37signals is moving programmers over, and the Command key is Super. The install is the dangerous part, not the keyboard.

Do this in order

  1. Do not wipe an M1–M4. Native Omarchy is Intel-only. Use Try Omarchy if you want a taste.
  2. T1 Touch Bar (2016–2017): keep macOS or you lose Touch Bar, webcam, and often audio. Details on Intel Macs.
  3. T2 (2018–2020): supported. Expect suspend quirks. Backup first. Encryption is the default.
  4. Flash the official ISO with balenaEtcher. Hold Option at boot, pick the orange EFI device. Verify the checksum.
  5. Give tiling two weeks. The only hotkey you must memorize is Super + K.

Apps you will miss

  • iMessage, FaceTime, Final Cut, Xcode — gone. Plan around that.
  • Microsoft Office: official Windows 11 VM has no GPU passthrough. Fine for Word, not for games.
  • Adobe: mostly the VM or web. Not a Photoshop workstation yet.
  • 1Password, Obsidian, Chromium, Neovim, Steam — first-class.
