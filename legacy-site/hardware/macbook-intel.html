Intel Macs

Official Mac support is Intel. The installer applies Broadcom, SPI keyboard, linux-t2, tiny-dfr, t2fanrd. Read T1 notes before a wipe. Apple Silicon is a different page.

bronze

MacBook Pro Touch Bar (T1, 2016–2017)

The T1 has no firmware in ROM. macOS writes EFI/APPLE/EMBEDDEDOS/combined.memboot. Wipe that and Touch Bar, webcam, ambient light, and often audio stay dead until macOS is reinstalled. Dual-boot or don’t.
