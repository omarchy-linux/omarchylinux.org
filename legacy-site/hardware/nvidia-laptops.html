NVIDIA laptops and desktops

Omarchy installs NVIDIA when it sees a supported GPU (open-dkms on Turing+, 580xx on Maxwell/Pascal/Volta). Muxed laptops are where people get hurt. asusctl is on the ISO.

Reddit: 4050-class units run well. Kill the dGPU for battery (envycontrol / asusctl).

Zephyrus G14 (2024, GA403, 4060)

Long 2026 write-up: second SSD install, dual kernel to flip mux, asusctl armoury, nvidia_drm.modeset=0 on the g14 kernel. Works; battery is a project.
