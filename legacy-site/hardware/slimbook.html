Slimbook (preinstalled)

DHH has pointed at Slimbook machines that ship with Omarchy already on the disk. Keyboard backlight for Tuxedo/Slimbook is patched in Omarchy itself.

  • This is the “I do not want to flash a USB” option.
  • Tuxedo is adjacent (same driver package: tuxedo-drivers-nocompatcheck-dkms) but Fedora-flavored Tuxedo OS is a different product.
  • Confirm the SKU and the preinstall with the vendor. This page is not a store.
